### Detailed Moment Breakdowns

#### MOMENT 1 — أسطورة الموزة (The Banana Myth)
**Timestamp**: 0:45–1:25
**Type**: Myth-bust
**Core Insight**: Common remedies like bananas, magnesium, and water are often scientifically insufficient for treating or preventing everyday muscle cramps.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: Starting with "خليني أصدمك بالواقع العلمي" (Let me shock you with the scientific reality) while showing a banana with a red "X" over it. It challenges a "universal truth" everyone grew up with.
**Curiosity Gap**: If it's not potassium or water, then what is it? Why do we keep getting them?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. The script already mentions this applies to "normal people," not those with chronic diseases (which is covered in the next segment of the full video).
**Platform Notes**: High text-overlay potential: "الموز مش هو الحل!" (Bananas aren't the solution!).

#### MOMENT 2 — كارت شحن العضلات (The ATP Energy Reveal)
**Timestamp**: 4:10–4:55
**Type**: Reveal / Practical Takeaway
**Core Insight**: Muscle relaxation isn't a passive process; it requires ATP (energy). When "credit" runs out, the muscle stays locked.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "إنت محتاج طاقة عشان العضلة تسيب بعضها!" (You need energy for the muscle to let go!). It's counter-intuitive—most people think relaxation is "doing nothing."
**Curiosity Gap**: How do we keep our "recharge card" full? What happens when the muscle stays locked too long?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit.
**Re-Edit Notes**: Needs to overlay the 3D animation of Actin/Myosin mentioned in the script to make the "Velcro" analogy clear visually.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It's a fundamental physiological fact.
**Platform Notes**: Use a "Battery Low" emoji or graphic to represent the ATP depletion.

#### MOMENT 3 — ليه السمانة بالذات؟ (The "Brake Pedal" Logic)
**Timestamp**: 6:30–7:15
**Type**: Reveal
**Core Insight**: If dehydration was the cause, your whole body would cramp. The fact that it's localized means it's a "nerve brake failure" in that specific muscle.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "لو الموضوع جفاف.. ليه جسمك كله مبيشدش في نفس الوقت؟" (If it's dehydration, why doesn't your whole body cramp at once?). A logical "gotcha" question that makes viewers rethink.
**Curiosity Gap**: What is this "brake pedal" (Golgi Tendon Organ) and how do I fix it?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It highlights the Neuromuscular Theory which is a leading scientific explanation.
**Platform Notes**: Split-screen showing a person drinking water vs. a person clutching their calf.

#### MOMENT 4 — نزيف جوه العضلة (The 3cm Hematoma)
**Timestamp**: 8:05–9:05
**Type**: Shocking Reveal
**Core Insight**: A cramp can be so violent it causes internal bleeding and a 3cm hematoma, explaining why the pain lasts for days.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: Showing the ultrasound image with the text "نزيف داخلي بسبب شد عضلي؟" (Internal bleeding due to a cramp?). The visual of the dark spot on the ultrasound is a powerful scroll-stopper.
**Curiosity Gap**: Can this happen to me? How do I know if my cramp caused a tear?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: **High Priority.** Must include the visual of the ultrasound and the explanation that this is why it hurts for days, but ensure it doesn't cause panic—frame it as "this is how strong your muscles are."
**Platform Notes**: Best for Facebook Reels (High drama/Health curiosity). Use captions for the medical terms (Hematoma/تجمع دموي).
