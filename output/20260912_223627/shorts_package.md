### Detailed Moment Breakdowns

#### MOMENT 1 — The Car Inspection Paradox
**Timestamp**: 0:00–0:45
**Type**: Reveal / Analogy
**Core Insight**: A resting heart (ECG) is like a parked car; it doesn't reveal how the "engine" behaves under the extreme stress of a match.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: The high-stakes comparison between a car inspection and a human life, delivered with Dr. Ahmed's serious but calm tone. 
**Curiosity Gap**: If the standard tests (ECG) aren't enough, what *actually* happens inside the heart during that fatal moment?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It accurately frames the limitation of resting ECGs without dismissing their general utility.
**Platform Notes**: Perfect for Reels. Use text overlays like "ليه رسم القلب مش كفاية؟" (Why is an ECG not enough?).

#### MOMENT 2 — The Vaccine Myth-Bust
**Timestamp**: 5:10–6:00
**Type**: Myth-bust
**Core Insight**: Sudden cardiac deaths aren't a "new" phenomenon caused by vaccines; they are simply more visible now due to 24/7 camera coverage and social media.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: Directly addresses a massive trending conspiracy theory ("نظريات المؤامرة واللقاحات") in the first 3 seconds.
**Curiosity Gap**: If it’s not the vaccines, then what is the actual biological "glitch" that doctors are seeing?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit. 
**Re-Edit Notes**: Start with the mention of vaccines, then immediately pivot to the "cameras everywhere" explanation. Ensure the conclusion (survival rates are actually higher now) is included for balance.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: **CRITICAL.** Must ensure the clip doesn't cut off before the scientific explanation to avoid being used by anti-vax groups.
**Platform Notes**: High engagement potential in comments. Expect heavy debate.

#### MOMENT 3 — The "Wall Growing Inward" (HCM)
**Timestamp**: 3:45–4:50
**Type**: "Wait, what?" Reveal
**Core Insight**: The most common cause of death (HCM) is a heart that gets "too strong" by growing its walls *inward*, leaving no room for blood.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: The counter-intuitive idea that a "strong" athlete's heart can be their downfall. Visualizing the "room being painted until it's too small" is a powerful mental image.
**Curiosity Gap**: How does a doctor tell the difference between a "healthy" strong heart and this "deadly" thick heart?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract (Best if the 3D heart animation is used).
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It explains a specific pathology clearly.
**Platform Notes**: Use "The Thick Wall" (الحيطة اللي بتكبر لجوه) as a recurring text hook.

#### MOMENT 4 — The Smoke Detector Trap
**Timestamp**: 7:15–8:15
**Type**: Practical / Shocking Stat
**Core Insight**: Full-body MRI scans for healthy people often create "False Positives"—medical false alarms that lead to unnecessary surgery and stress.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "ليه الأشعة الشاملة ممكن تكون أكبر فخ؟" (Why full-body scans might be a trap?). It challenges the "more money = more health" mindset.
**Curiosity Gap**: If these expensive scans are "traps," what should we be doing instead to stay safe?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It aligns with American College of Radiology (ACR) guidelines mentioned in the script.
**Platform Notes**: Great for Facebook Reels where the "middle-aged" demographic often considers these expensive "check-up packages."

#### MOMENT 5 — The "Million Dollar" Free Test
**Timestamp**: 10:15–11:00 (with intro hook)
**Type**: Practical Takeaway
**Core Insight**: A 10-minute conversation about family history is medically more valuable than a million-pound MRI scan.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "فحص مجاني تماماً.. أغلب مراكز الأشعة مش هتقولك عليه" (A totally free test... most scan centers won't tell you about).
**Curiosity Gap**: What are the specific questions I need to ask my family or tell my doctor? (Full video explains the "Gray Zone" and symptoms).
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit.
