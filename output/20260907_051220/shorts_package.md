**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit.

**Platform Notes**: High visual potential. Use the "Prepaid Card" graphic prominently in the vertical frame.

#### MOMENT 3 — The 3cm Internal Bleed

**Type**: Shocking Story / Stat
**Core Insight**: The physical force of a muscle cramp can be so intense that it ruptures blood vessels internally.
