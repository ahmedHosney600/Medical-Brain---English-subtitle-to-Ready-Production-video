#### MOMENT 1 — The Potassium Myth-Buster
**Timestamp**: 0:00–0:40
**Type**: Myth-bust
**Core Insight**: Common fixes like eating bananas or drinking water often have nothing to do with why a muscle actually cramps.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: Starting with the relatable "3 AM nightmare" pain, followed immediately by a "No" gesture to the most common advice (Potassium/Water).
**Curiosity Gap**: If it's not the salt or the water, what is actually "locking" the muscle?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It challenges a common (but often ineffective) home remedy without discouraging healthy habits.
**Platform Notes**: High engagement potential on Facebook Reels where "home remedies" are frequently shared.

**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "تخيلوا قوة الانقباض كانت شديدة لدرجة إنها سببت نزيف داخلي!" (Imagine the force was so strong it caused internal bleeding!).
**Curiosity Gap**: How do I know if my cramp caused an injury? Why is the pain lasting for days?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit
**Re-Edit Notes**: Needs a fast-paced intro hook: "هل الشد العضلي ممكن يسبب نزيف داخلي؟" (Can a cramp cause internal bleeding?) then cut to the 3cm hematoma story.

**Platform Notes**: Best for YouTube Shorts. Use the Ultrasound visual (from the script notes) as a "Picture-in-Picture" overlay.

#### MOMENT 3 — The "Gas & Brakes" Analogy
**Timestamp**: 5:45–6:40
**Type**: "Wait, what?" Reveal

**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract

**Platform Notes**: Needs "Gas" and "Brakes" emojis or icons appearing on screen to keep visual momentum.
