### Detailed Moment Breakdowns

#### MOMENT 1 — وسام القوة (The 42 BPM Myth-Bust)
**Timestamp**: 0:00–0:45 (Hook)
**Type**: Myth-bust / Reveal
**Core Insight**: A low heart rate notification (42 BPM) isn't always a medical emergency; for athletes, it’s a sign of high cardiac efficiency.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: Starting with a high-stakes scenario: "Imagine waking up to a red alert on your wrist saying your heart is stopping."
**Curiosity Gap**: Why would a "dangerously low" number actually be a "medal of honor" for your health?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: The clip must include the part where he mentions it's a "medal of honor" **but** only if you are healthy/athletic. The full video context is needed for those with symptoms, so the caption should read: "لو عندك دوخة أو إغماء، الوضع مختلف.. شوف الفيديو للآخر."
**Platform Notes**: Perfect for Reels. Use a red "42 BPM" text overlay that pulses like a warning light.

#### MOMENT 2 — سيلفي ولا كاميرا مراقبة؟ (The Data Revolution)
**Timestamp**: 3:00–4:15
**Type**: "Wait, what?" reveal
**Core Insight**: Traditional medical tests are "selfies" (snapshots), while smartwatches are "security cameras" (continuous data), changing how we diagnose disease.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: The visual analogy: "The difference between your lab test and your watch is the difference between a selfie and a 24-hour security camera."
**Curiosity Gap**: If the watch sees more than the doctor's test, which one should we trust?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit.
**Re-Edit Notes**: Combine the "Selfie vs. CCTV" explanation with the mention of the "Holter" monitor to show the evolution of tech.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It explains data collection methods rather than giving a diagnosis.
**Platform Notes**: Use split-screen visuals: a "Selfie" on one side and a "CCTV grid" on the other to reinforce the analogy.

#### MOMENT 3 — زغطة القلب (The Heart Hiccup)
**Timestamp**: 7:15–8:10
**Type**: Relatable / Practical
**Core Insight**: That "skipped beat" or "thump" feeling (ectopic beats) is often just a harmless "hiccup" that the watch makes you over-analyze.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "Have you ever felt like your heart 'missed a beat' or did a weird jump?"
**Curiosity Gap**: Is this feeling a sign of a coming heart attack, or just a normal body quirk?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Requires a text disclaimer: "هذا الكلام للحالات التي لا تعاني من أعراض عضوية أخرى" (This applies to cases without other organic symptoms).
**Platform Notes**: Very high shareability. People love knowing that a scary physical sensation is actually common and benign.

#### MOMENT 4 — وهم الخط الوهمي (The Passing Grade Analogy)
**Timestamp**: 1:45–2:45
**Type**: Myth-bust
**Core Insight**: "Normal" in medicine is an arbitrary statistical line (like a passing grade in school), not a divine law that applies perfectly to every individual.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "Is the student who gets 50% a genius and the one who gets 49.9% a failure? That's exactly how we set medical limits."
**Curiosity Gap**: If "Normal" is just an arbitrary line, how do I know if I'm actually sick?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit.
**Re-Edit Notes**: Start directly with the "Passing Grade" (درجة النجاح) analogy and cut to the explanation of the 10,000-person sample.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: High priority disclaimer: "الخطوط دي بتساعد الدكاترة ياخدوا قرار، مش عشان تشخص نفسك بنفسك." (These lines help doctors decide, not for self-diagnosis).
**Platform Notes**: Great for educational "Did you know?" style content on YouTube Shorts.
