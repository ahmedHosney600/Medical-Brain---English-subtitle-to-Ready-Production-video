### Detailed Moment Breakdowns

#### MOMENT 1 — كذبة الرقم "الطبيعي"
**Timestamp**: 1:30–2:25
**Type**: Myth-bust
**Core Insight**: Medical "normal" isn't a fixed truth; it's just the middle 95% of a "bread queue" of people.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "فيه مطب كبير بنقع فيه كلنا، وهو كلمة 'طبيعي'.. الطب مش مسطرة." This challenges the viewer's fundamental trust in medical charts.
**Curiosity Gap**: If "normal" is just an arbitrary statistical choice, how do I know if my specific heart rate is actually dangerous or just "off the curve"?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract. The transition from the "Bell Curve" to the "Bread Queue" (طابور العيش) is a perfect self-contained explanation.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It explains statistical methodology without telling people to ignore their doctors.
**Platform Notes**: High visual potential for the "Bell Curve" graphic to be overlaid in vertical format.

#### MOMENT 2 — ليه قلبك "بينط"؟
**Timestamp**: 5:10–6:05
**Type**: "Wait, what?" Reveal / Practical
**Core Insight**: The physical sensation of a "heart skip" (Ectopic) is actually the heart taking a compensatory pause and hitting harder on the next beat.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "تحس فجأة إن قلبك 'نط' أو خبط خبطة قوية في صدرك.. زي بوكسة في صدرك." Everyone has felt this; few know the mechanism.
**Curiosity Gap**: The clip explains *what* it is, but leaves the viewer wondering: "When does this 'normal' skip become a reason to see a cardiologist?"
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit. Needs to include the brief visual description of the "compensatory pause" (الوقفة التعويضية) to make sense.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: **CRITICAL.** Must include the spoken disclaimer or a text overlay: "لو حسيت معاها بدوخة أو ضيق نفس، لازم طبيب."
**Platform Notes**: Use SFX (heartbeat) to emphasize the "thump" described in the script.

#### MOMENT 3 — حكاية الدكتور والشهادة
**Timestamp**: 9:05–10:00
**Type**: Emotional / Reveal
**Core Insight**: Context matters more than numbers—a slow heart rate nearly ruined a career until the doctor proved it was just his personal "normal."
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "الزميل ده كان هيسقط في فحص الطيران بسبب نبضه.. وأنقذ نفسه بحركة بسيطة جداً."
**Curiosity Gap**: What was the "simple move" (walking around) and how many other "abnormal" readings are actually just healthy variations?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract. The story is told concisely and resolves the "hook" planted earlier.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. Illustrates the importance of clinical context over raw data.
**Platform Notes**: Best for Facebook Reels where storytelling and "human interest" medical stories perform exceptionally well.

#### MOMENT 4 — سبوبة أجهزة السكر
**Timestamp**: 7:00–7:55
**Type**: Myth-bust / Shocking Stat
**Core Insight**: Healthy influencers are using CGMs to create "content" out of normal sugar fluctuations, causing unnecessary health anxiety.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "ليه الإنفلونسرز فجأة بقوا مهووسين بجهاز السكر؟ هل الموضوع فعلاً عشان صحتنا، ولا مجرد 'تريند' بيبيع رعب؟"
**Curiosity Gap**: What does the "spectrum" of sugar actually look like for a healthy person, and why is the AI in these devices not ready for prime time?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit. Splice the "Influencer" critique with the explanation that sugar is a "spectrum" (Spectrum / طيف), not a fixed number.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It discourages medical "cosplay" by healthy individuals while acknowledging the device's value for actual diabetics.
**Platform Notes**: Very "shareable" for people tired of wellness-influencer culture.
