### Detailed Moment Breakdowns

#### MOMENT 1 — The Banana Myth
**Timestamp**: 0:00–0:55
**Type**: Myth-bust
**Core Insight**: Scientific studies show that athletes with cramps have the same electrolyte levels as those without; the "eat a banana" advice is largely a placebo.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: Starting with a banana in hand and saying "This isn't the solution" immediately challenges a universal "grandma's advice" health tip.
**Curiosity Gap**: If it's not the potassium or the salt, then what is actually happening inside my leg?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It clarifies that while bananas are healthy, they aren't a "rescue medication" for acute cramps.
**Platform Notes**: Perfect for Reels. Needs "الموز مش هو الحل" (Bananas aren't the solution) in bold text at the top.

#### MOMENT 2 — The 3cm Internal Bruise
**Timestamp**: 7:45–8:45
**Type**: Shocking Statistic / Reveal
**Core Insight**: A cramp is powerful enough to rupture blood vessels and cause a 3cm hematoma (tumble/clot) inside the muscle.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "Your muscle can literally tear itself apart while you sleep."
**Curiosity Gap**: How can a "simple" cramp be that violent? What does it look like on a scan?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit.
**Re-Edit Notes**: Needs a quick 2-second intro: "هل الشد العضلي مجرد وجع بسيط؟" (Is a cramp just a simple pain?) before jumping into the clinical case of the 3cm hematoma.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Must include a text overlay: "حالة نادرة ولكنها واردة" (A rare but possible case) to avoid mass panic, and remind viewers to see a doctor if pain persists for days.
**Platform Notes**: High visual potential if the editor adds a stock "Ultrasound" graphic or a red highlight on a leg diagram.

#### MOMENT 3 — The Stuck Doorbell
**Timestamp**: 6:15–7:15
**Type**: "Wait, what?" Reveal
**Core Insight**: The "Neuromuscular Hypothesis" — your internal "brakes" (Golgi Tendon Organs) fail, leaving the contraction signal "ringing" like a stuck doorbell.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: The analogy of the "Stuck Doorbell" (جرس باب علق) is highly relatable and explains a complex neurological failure simply.
**Curiosity Gap**: Why do the brakes fail? Is it just fatigue or something else?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It explains the mechanism without suggesting dangerous treatments.
**Platform Notes**: Use "Sound Effects" of a doorbell ringing during the explanation to increase retention.

#### MOMENT 4 — The Energy to Relax (ATP)
**Timestamp**: 4:30–5:15
**Type**: Counter-intuitive Reveal
**Core Insight**: Muscles require "Energy Cards" (ATP) to *un-link* fibers. When you run out of energy, the muscle stays "stuck" in the on position.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "عشان عضلاتك تفك، محتاجة طاقة.. مش بس عشان تشد" (To relax, your muscles need energy... not just to contract).
**Curiosity Gap**: How do I get that energy back quickly? Does stretching help?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit.
**Re-Edit Notes**: Needs a fast-paced edit showing the "Actin/Myosin" team-up and the "Prepaid Card" (ATP) failing.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. Explains basic physiology.
**Platform Notes**: Great for "Educational" niche on YouTube Shorts. Use "Battery" or "Charging" icons visually.
