#### MOMENT 1 — "The Million-Dollar Paradox"
**Timestamp**: 0:00–0:55
**Type**: Emotional / Wait, what? Reveal
**Core Insight**: Even the most expensive medical equipment can't always predict a sudden cardiac arrest in elite athletes.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: Dr. Ahmed’s calm but heavy opening: "إزاي أقوى قلب في الدنيا ممكن يقف فجأة كدة؟" combined with the mention of local tragedies like Ahmed Refaat.

**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It frames the issue as a medical mystery/complexity rather than a failure of medicine itself.
**Platform Notes**: High emotional resonance for Facebook Reels (Egyptian audience). Use captions for the names of the players mentioned.

#### MOMENT 2 — "The Car Inspection (Fahs el-Morour)"
**Timestamp**: 3:30–4:30
**Type**: Practical / Counter-intuitive
**Core Insight**: A medical "pass" is a snapshot of the present, not a guarantee of the future—just like a car passing its annual inspection.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: The visual of a car inspection vs. a heart monitor. "الفحص الطبي هو لقطة للواقع.. مش ضمان للمستقبل."
**Curiosity Gap**: If the standard "pass" isn't a guarantee, what should we actually be looking for?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract (requires the car animation from the script to be overlaid).
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Excellent. It manages expectations about what medical tests can and cannot do.
**Platform Notes**: Perfect for YouTube Shorts. The analogy is "sticky" and easy to share.

#### MOMENT 3 — "The Scarecrow (Khayal Ma'ata)"
**Timestamp**: 5:30–6:30 (and parts of 7:45)
**Type**: Myth-busting / Shocking Stat
**Core Insight**: Full-body scans often find "shadows" (false positives) that lead to unnecessary anxiety and surgeries.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "الشركات دي بتلعب على أغلى حاجة عندك: خوفك على نفسك."
**Curiosity Gap**: Why does the American College of Radiology warn against these scans? What happens when a scan says you're "fine" but you're not?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit. Combine the "Khayal Ma'ata" analogy with the mention of the ACR (American College of Radiology) statement for authority.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Crucial context needed—must keep the part where Dr. Ahmed says "I'm not saying don't check, I'm saying check right."
**Platform Notes**: Very "viral" potential on Reels due to the critique of a popular "wellness trend."

#### MOMENT 4 — "The Most Important Free Test"
**Timestamp**: 9:15–10:15
**Type**: Practical Takeaway
**Core Insight**: Your family history is a more powerful diagnostic tool than a $3,000 MRI.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "أهم فحص ممكن تعملوه ببلاش.. الإجابة على السؤال ده أهم عند دكتور القلب من 100 إشعة."
