### Detailed Moment Breakdowns

#### MOMENT 1 — The Banana Myth
**Timestamp**: 0:00–0:55
**Type**: Myth-bust
**Core Insight**: Common advice like eating bananas or drinking water doesn't actually stop cramps because they are risk factors, not the direct biological trigger.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: Starting with the relatable midnight scream and immediately debunking the "Banana/Water" solution that everyone grew up hearing.
**Curiosity Gap**: If it’s not the water or the potassium, then what *is* actually happening inside my leg?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract. The hook is already written to be a perfect standalone intro.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It correctly identifies them as "Risk Factors" rather than "Direct Causes" without telling people to stop eating healthy.
**Platform Notes**: Excellent for Reels. Use text overlays: "الموز مش هو الحل؟" (Is the banana not the solution?).

#### MOMENT 2 — The Relaxation Paradox
**Timestamp**: 4:10–5:05
**Type**: "Wait, what?" Reveal
**Core Insight**: Muscles require energy (ATP) to *detach* and relax; a cramp is essentially your muscle "running out of credit" to stop contracting.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "Did you know your body needs energy to *stop* moving just as much as it needs it to start?"
**Curiosity Gap**: How does a "prepaid card" (ATP) control my pain, and what happens when it runs out?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit. Needs a quick intro sentence like "Have you ever wondered why a cramp won't let go?" followed by the ATP analogy.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It’s a fundamental physiological fact (Actin-Myosin cycle).
**Platform Notes**: Needs the "Sliding Door" or "Prepaid Card" visual graphics from the main video to be effective in vertical format.

#### MOMENT 3 — The 3cm Internal Bleeding
**Timestamp**: 8:40–9:35
**Type**: Shocking Reveal
**Core Insight**: The force of a muscle cramp is so powerful it can physically tear tissue and cause a 3cm internal hematoma.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: Showing the ultrasound graphic with the text "نزيف داخلي بسبب شد عضلي؟" (Internal bleeding due to a cramp?).
**Curiosity Gap**: Why is my nervous system allowing my muscle to exert enough force to injure itself?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract. The transition into the clinical case is very punchy.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: **High Priority.** Must include a caption or voiceover context that this is an extreme/severe case so viewers don't think every minor cramp is a medical emergency.
**Platform Notes**: High viral potential on Facebook Reels due to the "shock" factor. Use a red circle on the ultrasound thumbnail.

#### MOMENT 4 — The "Big Sports Drink" Theory
**Timestamp**: 6:00–6:45
**Type**: Reveal / Controversy
**Core Insight**: The popular "Dehydration Theory" was heavily funded by sports drink companies to sell products, despite scientific gaps.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "Follow the money: Why do we all believe cramps are about salt?"
**Curiosity Gap**: If the salt theory is just marketing, what is the *real* scientific theory? (Leads to the Neuromuscular Theory in the full video).
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit. Requires a punchy opening: "ليه بنصدق إن الحل في إزازة مية بملح؟"
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It raises a valid point about scientific funding (Conflict of Interest) without telling people to stop hydrating.
**Platform Notes**: Great for "Educational/Explainer" style Shorts. Use fast-paced cuts between Dr. Ahmed and sports drink icons.
