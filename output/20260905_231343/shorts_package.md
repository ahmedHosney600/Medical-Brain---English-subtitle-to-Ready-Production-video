### Detailed Moment Breakdowns

#### MOMENT 1 — لغز الأكسجين القاتل
**Timestamp**: 0:00–0:55
**Type**: Paradox / Reveal
**Core Insight**: The very thing that saves a heart attack patient (oxygen/blood flow) can trigger a "second hit" that destroys heart cells.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: Starts with a powerful analogy of a thirsty man dying because he drank water. It contradicts the universal belief that "oxygen = always good."
**Curiosity Gap**: How can oxygen be a poison? Why does "success" in surgery lead to the "beginning of the end"?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: The hook is followed immediately by the explanation that this happens *during* surgery. It’s safe because it frames it as a medical mystery, not a reason to avoid oxygen.
**Platform Notes**: High energy, needs "Text Overlays" for the paradox (e.g., الأكسجين = سم؟).

#### MOMENT 2 — كارثة الميكروباص (No Reflow)
**Timestamp**: 5:40–6:45
**Type**: Practical / Reveal
**Core Insight**: Explains "No Reflow" — why opening the main artery doesn't always restore blood flow to the small vessels due to cellular swelling.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: The relatable Egyptian analogy of a "crowded microbus in the sun" and "onlookers blocking the road after an accident."
**Curiosity Gap**: If the doctor put in the stent (the "tow truck"), why is the road still blocked? What are these "swollen cells"?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit. 
**Re-Edit Notes**: Needs to cut directly to the "Microbus" analogy and use the animation from the script to visualize the "swelling" (Edema).
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Must include the line "The doctor knows how to handle this" to avoid inducing panic in heart patients.
**Platform Notes**: Perfect for Facebook Reels where "relatable analogies" perform very well with older demographics.

#### MOMENT 3 — حركة الدراع السحرية
**Timestamp**: 7:15–8:10
**Type**: "Wait, what?" Reveal
**Core Insight**: Remote Ischemic Preconditioning — the idea that temporarily cutting blood to the arm can trigger a protective chemical response in the heart.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: Visual of Dr. Ahmed putting a blood pressure cuff on his arm while talking about "vaccinating the heart." It looks like a "life hack."
**Curiosity Gap**: Does this actually work? Can I do this at home to prevent a heart attack? (The full video explains why it failed in human trials).
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: **CRITICAL.** Must keep the part where he says "this was a miracle in animals" so people don't start doing this at home as a treatment. Add a caption: "العلم وراء حيلة الدراع" (The science behind the arm trick).
**Platform Notes**: Very high "shareability" because it looks like a health secret.

#### MOMENT 4 — سبوبة تنظيف الشرايين
**Timestamp**: 9:15–10:15
**Type**: Myth-busting / Shock
**Core Insight**: Most supplements marketed for "cleaning arteries" or "antioxidants" are useless in the face of real medical emergencies like reperfusion injury.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: Direct attack on "Wellness Gurus" and the "800-dollar devices." People love seeing scams exposed.
**Curiosity Gap**: Why don't vitamins work? What does "real science" say about cleaning arteries?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It discourages reliance on unproven supplements over professional medical care.
**Platform Notes**: Use aggressive captions: "كذبة مكملات الشرايين" or "الفرق بين العلم والسبوبة."
