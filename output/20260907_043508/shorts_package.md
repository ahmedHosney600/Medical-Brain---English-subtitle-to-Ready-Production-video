### Detailed Moment Breakdowns

#### MOMENT 1 — The 3 AM Internal Bleeding
**Timestamp**: 0:00–0:55
**Type**: Shocking Reveal / Medical Mystery
**Core Insight**: Muscle cramps aren't just painful; in extreme cases, they can cause physical trauma and internal bleeding (Hematoma).
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: Starts with "Imagine a simple cramp... cutting your blood vessels from the inside." This creates immediate high-stakes tension.
**Curiosity Gap**: If a cramp is that powerful, *why* does the body allow it to happen? What are these proteins (Actin/Myosin) that "get into a fight"?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract. The hook is already written for high retention.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It cites a specific medical case (UK doctor) and frames it as a "real medical case," preventing general panic while highlighting the severity.
**Platform Notes**: Needs a text overlay: "الشد العضلي ممكن يقطع عروقك؟" (Can a cramp cut your veins?).

#### MOMENT 2 — The Microbus & Prepaid Card
**Timestamp**: 2:15–3:15
**Type**: Educational / Analogy
**Core Insight**: Explaining how muscles contract and why they get "stuck" using the analogy of a microbus driver and a prepaid electricity card.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "تخيل الميوزين ده سواق ميكروباص.." (Imagine Myosin is a microbus driver). Using a very Egyptian, non-medical analogy for a complex biological process.
**Curiosity Gap**: What happens when the "prepaid card" (ATP) runs out? Why does the driver stay "clutched" to the gear?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit. Needs to be spliced with the animation mentioned in the script (driver/gear) to make the visual match the audio for vertical viewing.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It's a simplified physiological explanation.
**Platform Notes**: High visual potential. Use split-screen: Dr. Ahmed on top, animation on bottom.

#### MOMENT 3 — The Magnesium Myth-Bust
**Timestamp**: 6:50–7:45
**Type**: Myth-Busting / Practical
**Core Insight**: Common remedies like magnesium and bananas have very weak scientific evidence for preventing cramps.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "مفيش دليل علمي قاطع إن الماغنسيوم أو الموز بيمنع الكرامبات." (There is no definitive scientific evidence that magnesium or bananas prevent cramps). This directly contradicts "Mom's advice."
**Curiosity Gap**: If the popular stuff doesn't work, what *actually* does? (Leads to the "Exercise" solution in the full video).
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: **CRITICAL.** Must include the part where he says "this isn't a substitute for a doctor" or add a text disclaimer: "استشر طبيبك لو الكرامبات متكررة."
**Platform Notes**: Use "X" emojis over photos of bananas and magnesium bottles to visually signal the myth-busting.

#### MOMENT 4 — The "Short Circuit" (Nerve Theory)
**Timestamp**: 5:10–6:05
**Type**: "Wait, what?" Reveal
**Core Insight**: Cramps might not be a "muscle" problem, but a "glitch" in the spinal cord's electrical signals.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "الموضوع مش جفاف.. الموضوع قفلة كهرباء في النخاع الشوكي." (It's not dehydration; it's a short circuit in the spinal cord).
**Curiosity Gap**: Why would the spinal cord send a "contract" signal instead of an "easy" signal? How do we stop this "doorbell" from ringing?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit. Requires a quick intro sentence like: "Ever wondered why only one leg cramps if you're dehydrated?" to set the context before explaining the theory.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It's presented as the "Neuromuscular Hypothesis," which is the leading scientific theory.
**Platform Notes**: Use a "Glitch" sound effect and visual filter when mentioning the "short circuit."

### Recommended Extraction Order

1. **Moment 1 (The Injury)** — This is the strongest "Viral" hook. It uses shock value and a "medical mystery" vibe that performs exceptionally well on Reels/Shorts.
2. **Moment 3 (The Myth-Bust)** — This will generate the most comments/engagement. People will argue in the comments about whether magnesium worked for them, which boosts the algorithm.
3. **Moment 2 (The Analogy)** — Great for "Save"ability. People save educational content that makes them feel smart or explains things simply.
