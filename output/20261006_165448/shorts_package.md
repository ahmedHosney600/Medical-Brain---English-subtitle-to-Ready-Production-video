### Detailed Moment Breakdowns

#### MOMENT 1 — The Athlete Paradox
**Timestamp**: 4:15–5:05 in long-form script
**Type**: Wait, what? / Counter-intuitive
**Core Insight**: Medical "Normal" is a statistical average, not a biological law—proven by athletes whose healthy heart rates would trigger medical alarms.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "هل كنت تعرف إن فيه رياضيين عالميين... الساعة ممكن تطلب لهم الإسعاف فوراً؟" (Did you know world-class athletes could have their watches call an ambulance right now?)
**Curiosity Gap**: If a "dangerously low" heart rate is actually a sign of peak fitness, how do I know if *my* heart rate is actually a problem or just my "normal"?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe, as it explicitly mentions these are "world-class athletes" and highlights that the watch follows "blind statistics."
**Platform Notes**: Perfect for Reels. Use a split screen: Top (Athlete training), Bottom (Dr. Ahmed explaining).

#### MOMENT 2 — The "Perfect Result" Trap
**Timestamp**: 8:45–9:45 in long-form script
**Type**: Myth-busting / Shocking Reveal
**Core Insight**: A "Normal" lab result (like blood sugar) doesn't account for the massive effort (insulin) the body might be making to keep it there.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "ليه ممكن نتيجة تحليلك تطلع 'مثالية' وبالمللي في النطاق الطبيعي، ومع ذلك تكون دي بداية المشكلة؟" (Why your result could be "perfect" and yet be the start of the problem?)
**Curiosity Gap**: What is the hidden factor that the lab test isn't showing me? (Leads to the full explanation of insulin resistance).
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract
**Medical Responsibility Check**: High importance. Must ensure the "Insulin vs. Glucose" explanation is kept intact so people don't start distrusting all labs without reason.
**Platform Notes**: Text overlay "فخ التحليل السليم" (The Healthy Test Trap) is essential for sound-off viewers.

#### MOMENT 3 — Heart "Hiccups" (Ectopics)
**Timestamp**: 7:45–8:45 in long-form script
**Type**: Practical / Relatable
**Core Insight**: Most "skipped beats" felt by people are harmless "Ectopics" (heart hiccups), but smartwatches often mislabel them as dangerous irregularities.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "الضربات دي عاملة زي 'الزغطة' البسيطة في وسط الكلام." (These beats are like a simple 'hiccup' in the middle of a sentence.)
**Curiosity Gap**: If my watch says "Irregular Rhythm," is it a "hiccup" or a real heart issue?
**Extraction Complexity**: Needs Re-Edit
**Re-Edit Notes**: Needs a punchier 2-second intro like: "بتحس قلبك 'نط' فجأة من مكانه؟" (Feel your heart suddenly 'jump'?) before moving into the explanation.
**Medical Responsibility Check**: **CRITICAL.** Must include the disclaimer: "لو الحالة متكررة لازم استشارة طبيب." (If it's repetitive, consult a doctor.)
**Platform Notes**: Use "Heartbeat" SFX and a visual of a "Hiccup" text bubble to make the analogy stick.

#### MOMENT 4 — 100,000 vs. 10
**Timestamp**: 2:15–3:10 in long-form script
**Type**: Shocking Statistic
**Core Insight**: A doctor's ECG sees 10 heartbeats; a smartwatch sees 100,000. This creates a "Security Camera" effect that reveals details we never used to see.
**Scroll-Stopper Factor**: "الساعة بتشوف حوالي 100 ألف نبضة قلب في اليوم، بينما رسم القلب العادي في العيادة بيشوف منهم 10 نبضات بس!"
**Curiosity Gap**: Does more data mean better health, or just more anxiety?
**Extraction Complexity**: Clean Extract
**Medical Responsibility Check**: Safe. It frames the technology as a "Security Camera," which is a neutral, helpful metaphor.
**Platform Notes**: High visual potential. Use on-screen counters (10 vs 100,000) to emphasize the scale.
