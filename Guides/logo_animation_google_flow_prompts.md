## Prompt 1: Full Intro Animation (3 seconds)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> In Google Flow, upload the reference image above as your input image, set duration to **4 seconds** (Google Flow minimum), and paste the prompt below.

### Google Flow Prompt (copy-paste ready):

```
On a dark navy background (#0A1628), a single glowing cyan line (#00B4D8) 
begins drawing itself from the left edge of the screen. The line traces a 
smooth ECG heartbeat waveform with two clean peaks as it travels to the 
right. The line behaves like a glowing comet trail — the bright leading tip 
draws forward while the earlier part of the trail gradually fades away 
behind it. As the line curves upward and begins drawing a circle outline at 
the center of the frame, the initial heartbeat waveform is already fading 
out and dissolving into the dark background. The line keeps flowing in one 
unbroken stroke — it completes the circle and then continues drawing inward, 
tracing an ECG heartbeat peak and the Arabic letter "ح" inside the circle. 
By the time the inner symbol is complete, only the circle and the symbol 
inside it remain glowing — the earlier heartbeat trail has fully vanished. 
The completed logo pulses with a brief luminous glow. Then white text 
"Dr Ahmed Hosney" and Arabic text "د. أحمد حسني" fade in smoothly below 
the logo with a gentle upward slide. The final frame holds. Clean, minimal, 
premium, cinematic. Smooth motion throughout. No particles, no explosions.
```

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Try all 3 main prompts** — generate 3-4 variations of each, pick the best
2. **If the text comes out wrong** (AI often struggles with Arabic text), generate the animation WITHOUT text, then add the text in Premiere Pro as a separate layer on top
3. **Layer approach**: Generate just the ECG-line-to-circle animation, then composite the text in Premiere. This gives you the cleanest result.
4. **SFX to add in Premiere afterwards:**
   - Heartbeat monitor beep (2 clean beeps) — search Freesound: "ECG beep clean"
   - Subtle deep whoosh at logo reveal — search: "cinematic logo whoosh soft"
   - Optional: faint A minor piano note underneath — search: "piano note A minor sustain"
