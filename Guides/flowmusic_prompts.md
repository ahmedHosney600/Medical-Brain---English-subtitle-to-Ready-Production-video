## Your Music DNA (What We're Going For)

Your content is **medical detective storytelling** — you uncover mysteries about the human body with the warmth of a trusted friend and the precision of a scientist. Your references:

- **Theme for Villain** (Joe Kraemer) → Dark, rhythmic piano waltz with intellectual tension
- **No.1 A Minor Waltz** (Esther Abrami) → Elegant, melancholic solo piano, contemplative
- **الدحيح vibes** → Signature sound that's distinctive but NEVER overpowers speech

**Core rules for all tracks:**
- Must sit comfortably at -18dB to -22dB under speech without losing character
- No lyrics, no vocals, no humming
- No generic "happy corporate" or "upbeat tech startup" sounds
- Piano is the signature instrument — it should anchor every track

## 🎛️ Generation Tips for FlowMusic

1. **Generate 5-8 variations** of each prompt and pick the best one
2. **Test at -20dB** under your voice recording before choosing — a track that sounds great solo might sound muddy under speech
3. **Check loop points** — Beds 1, 3, and 5 need to loop cleanly for long sections
4. **Key consistency** — Try to keep Beds 1, 2, and 6 in **A minor** so they transition smoothly into each other
5. **Layer test** — Play Bed 1 (Investigation) and Bed 4 (Reveal) back to back. The Reveal should feel like a dramatic escalation of the Investigation, not a completely different song
